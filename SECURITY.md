# Security policy

Bulkhead is pre-release (0.x). A green verify report means no known leak paths, not proof of none.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability").

## Response window

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- Initial assessment within 10 business days.
- Fix or mitigation plan communicated after assessment; coordinated disclosure by agreement.

## Scope

Anything that breaks a rule in `SPEC.md`, especially tenant isolation, authorization, and encryption rules. Please include the rule ID and a reproduction.
